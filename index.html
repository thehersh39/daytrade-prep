Morning Day-Trade Prep

Built around a tight stop. Every plan below uses a stop no wider than your limit, sizes shares so a loss equals your per-trade risk, and ranks stocks by whether their normal daily swing gives a stop that size a fair chance.

Tight stops need intraday entriesA 1.5-point stop only works if you enter right at the level, on a 1- or 5-minute candle that breaks and holds. Chasing a move that has already gone 1 point uses up most of your stop before the trade starts.
Pick names whose range fitsA stock that swings 5 points a day gives a 1.5-point stop room to breathe. One that swings 50 points will hit that stop on noise. The fit rating handles this for you.

Earnings, analyst rating changes since the last close, and names gapping 3%+ premarket or that moved 5%+ last session, with the headline behind it.

Earnings this week

Ranges widen into a report. With a tight stop, avoid holding anything into the close on its report day.
